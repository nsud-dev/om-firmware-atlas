## Different sources answer different questions.

The Atlas connects independent work. Firmware builds, emulators and look editors stay in their own projects. This first catalogue covers a selected set of reviewed sources; missing entries mean there is more to catalogue.

</div>

<div class="evidence-trio" markdown="1">

<div class="evidence-card" markdown="1">
<span class="evidence-tag">Published release</span>

A project made something available. Test coverage is a separate question.

</div>

<div class="evidence-card" markdown="1">
<span class="evidence-tag">Emulator observation</span>

Specific behaviour under the named emulator, revision, model and scenario.

</div>

<div class="evidence-card" markdown="1">
<span class="evidence-tag">Physical-camera report</span>

What a reporter says happened on the identified camera and package.

</div>

</div>
