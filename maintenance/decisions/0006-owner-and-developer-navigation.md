# 0006 — Navigation for camera owners and developers

Updated by [0007 — Direct routes for owners and developers](0007-direct-owner-and-developer-routes.md).

The two visitor routes have different starting points. Camera owners choose their exact model, see its release and test status, then open its showcase for project highlights, screenshots and install guides. Developers choose between cross-body research and a directory of model-specific firmware builds, projects, findings and tests.

Camera overviews and project entries retain their existing URLs. The generator builds each model's overview, showcase and research page from the same camera and project records. Each showcase groups highlights under the project responsible for them, and release links use the existing model-specific selection and limitations. Multi-model project images require a recorded model to appear in a camera showcase. For example, the Caduceus OM-5 Mark II screenshots and install guide appear in the Mark II showcase, while the original OM-5 showcase lists its port status.

The top-level navigation lists the two audiences. Camera, project and guide lists live on their directory pages. Release news and testing requests are on the owner camera directory. The evidence guide and community pages carry the supporting information previously on the homepage.
